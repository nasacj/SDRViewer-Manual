Connect in the app

rtl_tcp is running on the computer. What is left is telling the app where it is.

Before you start, two things: the rtl_tcp window says listening..., and the phone and the computer are on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. Open the settings

    Go to the Receiver page (the second tab at the bottom) and tap the gear at the top left.

  2. Edit the receiver

    The first row of the settings is Receiver, with the current receiver's name on the right. Tap it and choose Edit… from the menu.

  3. Fill in the address
    FieldWhat to enter
    NameAnything that helps you recognise it, such as "RTL-SDR in the study".
    TCP / UDPTCP. An ordinary rtl_tcp speaks TCP.
    HostThe computer's address from the install step, for example 192.168.1.20. For a Raspberry Pi a name such as raspberrypi.local works too.
    Port1234, unless you started rtl_tcp with -p.
  4. Press play

    Back on the page, tap the play button to the left of the frequency. Within a few seconds the waterfall starts to scroll: you are connected. The easiest proof is FM broadcast on the Radio page; see Radio.

Connected, but nothing arrives? The usual cause is that rtl_tcp serves one client at a time: if another SDR program on the computer, or another phone, is connected, a later one connects and receives nothing. For the rest see If it will not connect.

Frequency calibration (Crystal error)

The crystal in an inexpensive receiver is not exact: tuned to 100.000 MHz it may really be ten or more kilohertz away. FM broadcast hides that, but narrow signals such as air traffic, APRS and AIS are simply not there at that error. The app measures the error and corrects it.

ModeHow to use it
Automatic (recommended)Nothing to set. On the Radio page, listen to a few stereo FM stations for a few seconds each. The app measures the error from each station's pilot tone and applies it once three stations agree.
ManualIf you already know this receiver's error in ppm, type it.
  • An RTL-SDR Blog V3, V4 or V4L has an accurate crystal, usually within 1–2 ppm; the default is fine.
  • A generic TV dongle can be tens of ppm out, or more than a hundred. Calibrate it once.
  • After changing to another receiver, tap Measure again. Every receiver has its own error.
  • A new value takes effect at the next connection: stop and play again.

More than one receiver

Add receiver… in the Receiver menu adds another entry. Each page (Radio, Receiver and ATC as one group; Aviation, APRS, AIS and ADS-B each on their own) chooses its receiver in its own settings:

  • Pages on the same receiver take turns: starting one stops the other.
  • Pages on different receivers run together, for example air traffic audio while the ADS-B page shows the aircraft.

Editing an entry's address changes it for every page that uses it.
