If it will not connect

Find the line that matches what you see. Most problems are in the network and the address, not in the receiver.

A quick check first

  1. Is rtl_tcp running?Its window should say listening..., and client accepted! once the phone connects.
  2. Is it the same network?The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi. Guest and office networks often stop devices from reaching each other.
  3. Is the address right?A computer's address can change when the router hands out a new one. Look it up again and compare it with the app.

By symptom

What you seeMost likelyWhat to do
Play never connects, or reports a failureWrong address or port; a firewall; rtl_tcp started without -a 0.0.0.0Check the address and port 1234; on Windows check the firewall allows rtl_tcp; make sure the command has -a 0.0.0.0
Connected, but the waterfall is still and there is no soundrtl_tcp is already serving another program or deviceClose other SDR software on the computer, or restart rtl_tcp
Nothing arrives after the receiver was unplugged and plugged backrtl_tcp does not notice its receiver going and keeps using a dead handleStop rtl_tcp and run it again
The waterfall moves but shows only noise, no stationsThe antenna is not connected; unsuitable gain; an old driver for a V4 or V4LCheck the antenna connector; run rtl_test -t on the computer and see the model is recognised
FM plays, air traffic or APRS not at allThe receiver's frequency is not calibratedSee Frequency calibration: listen to a few stereo FM stations first
Audio breaks up and the drop count on the page risesWeak or busy Wi-Fi: samples are lost on the wayMove closer to the router; put the computer on a cable; lower the sample rate to 1.024 MSPS
Audio is dull or distortedThe calibration belongs to a different receiverTap Measure again in the receiver's editor
A name such as xxx.local does not connectThe network does not resolve namesUse the numeric address, for example 192.168.1.20
After switching to another app and back, a page that makes no sound has stoppediOS suspends apps in the background; only a page playing audio keeps runningIt reconnects by itself on return, and the page says for how long it was paused

What the numbers on the page mean

The Receiver page has a line of small figures above the spectrum, which is useful when something is wrong:

NameMeaningWhen all is well
linkThe sample rate actually arrivingClose to the rate you set
iqdropSamples the phone could not process in time0
underTimes the speaker ran out of audio0 or a few
ringHow full the audio buffer isMoving a little around 25%

If link is well below the rate you set, the problem is in the network or on the computer. If iqdrop rises, the phone is too busy: lower the sample rate.

Still stuck

Three things let the author find the problem fastest: the computer's system and the receiver's model; the output of rtl_test -t; and a screenshot of the page with the line of figures above.
